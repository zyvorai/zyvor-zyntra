# Leadership pack

Weekly review for a B2B leadership team. The sample week is the ₹20 lakh question: spend on marketing, hire two engineers, stage one hire and a smaller campaign, postpone, or do nothing.

The model makes the constraint explicit. `spend_20l_marketing` consumes onboarding capacity, and an invariant blocks it while that KPI would worsen. Hiring raises capacity first. A landing experiment is a separate, low-risk action, not a budget decision.

Numbers come from `kpis.yaml` and, once you bind them, from the exports in `sources.example.yaml`. The simulator ranks. The executive overlay in `executive.yaml` only names the decision, the audience, and the assumptions a reviewer can turn off.

Owners: `ceo`, `cto`, `marketing`, `sales`. Timezone: Asia/Kolkata.
